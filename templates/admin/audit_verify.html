Every activity and file-access log entry is chained by hash to the one before it — entry_hash = SHA-256(content + previous entry_hash). Editing, deleting, or reordering a historical row breaks the chain from that point forward. This re-walks both chains end to end and recomputes every hash to prove (or disprove) that the audit trail itself hasn't been tampered with.
